Method & sources

Ratios

GPUs per BTC = BTC/USD daily close ÷ GPU price in USD. Sats per GPU is the inverse × 100,000,000. At MSRP holds the launch price constant, so the line moves only with bitcoin and reaches back to each card's launch day.

Indexed rescales every line to 100 at its first visible point, which makes cards with very different prices comparable.

Data

  • BTC/USD: Binance BTCUSDT daily close (Coinbase fallback)
  • New retail: gpupricehistory.com, US daily min and average across retailers, from Jan 2026
  • Used / sold: pcprice.watch, eBay completed sales, 90-day median
  • MSRP and launch dates: manufacturer announcements

A GitHub Action refreshes this daily and the archive only grows, so history reaches past each source's rolling window. Not financial advice. Prices are US only and exclude tax.
